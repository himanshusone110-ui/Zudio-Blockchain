💊 How ZudioCoin Works

ZudioCoin provides continuous mathematical bonding curve liquidity for meme coins on the ZUDIO blockchain.

1
Pick a coin: Browse meme coins in the Newest, Bonding, and Curve full columns.
2
Buy on the bonding curve: Every purchase adds confirmed ZDC to the curve and increases spot price according to k = zdc_reserve * token_reserve.
3
Sell anytime: Sell your tokens back to the curve whenever you want with 0 trade fee.
4
Curve full at 50,000 ZDC: When confirmed ZDC reaches 50,000 ZDC, the coin displays "curve full" and buying is disabled.
